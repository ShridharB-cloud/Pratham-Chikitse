First Aid at Your Fingertips

A bilingual, offline-first Android application designed to provide immediate life-saving instructions for 20+ emergency scenarios in both Kannada and English.

Explore Source Code Live Demo Preview

Why Pratham-Chikitse?

🌍

Bilingual Support

Fully accessible in Kannada and English, ensuring critical information reaches everyone in their preferred language.

📴

Offline First

Zero internet required. All instructions, assets, and voice guides are bundled within the app for remote area reliability.

🔊

Voice Guidance

Integrated Text-to-Speech (TTS) provides step-by-step audio instructions, allowing responders to keep their eyes on the patient.

📍

Hospital Finder

Quickly locate nearby medical facilities with integrated mapping and emergency contact shortcuts.
